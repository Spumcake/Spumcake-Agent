## Prompt

You are writing the shot list for "{{title}}", an animated film of {{length}}, from its approved script below: every shot, in order, with how long it lasts and what the camera does. Boards will draw one frame per shot, and an animatic will time them to your lengths.

For each shot give:

- **Shot:** the script's scene number, a hyphen, and the shot's place in the scene, such as `3-1`, `3-2`, or `3A-1` in an added scene;
- **Length:** in whole seconds. Time a spoken line at about two and a half words a second, plus a short pause before and after. Most shots run 2 to 8 seconds; one longer than 10 needs a reason, given in its action;
- **Framing:** wide, medium, close-up, or extreme close-up, and how many characters are in it;
- **Angle:** eye level, low, high, overhead, or over the shoulder;
- **Camera:** one move, or still: push in, pull out, pan, tilt, truck, or track, with how far and how fast when it matters. Never more than one move in a shot;
- **Action:** what happens in the shot, in a sentence;
- **Lines:** the spoken lines it covers, by speaker, quoted word for word from the script, each marked *on screen* when the speaker's mouth is seen in this shot, or *off screen* when it is not, such as a line heard over another character's reaction;
- **Characters and props, and place:** by the names the script lists, and only those.

Every action and every spoken line of the script falls in exactly one shot. Open each scene on a shot that shows where it is, unless the camera notes or the script say otherwise. The lengths add up to {{length}}, within a few seconds. Characters who read as minors stay in ordinary, everyday situations; nothing about them is sexual or suggestive.
