# Location brief: {{name}}

Fill every field from the user's request, the art, and the scenes set here. Write "not specified" rather than inventing. Keep it short: every image of the place is prompted from this brief, and anything not here should not appear in them.

## The place

- **Name:** as the script uses it
- **What it is:** one line, such as "a cramped farmhouse kitchen, lived in for fifty years"
- **Scenes set here:** by the script's or shot list's numbers, with what happens in each
- **What it must convey:** three to five words

## Layout

- **Shape and size:** the overall shape, and its size against a known subject, such as "ceiling a little over twice Mira's height"
- **Areas:** each area by name, such as "the stove corner", and what is in it
- **Openings:** every door, window, and stair, by the wall it is on, and where it leads
- **Fixed furniture:** what never moves, and where it stands
- **Where people move:** entrances and exits, and the paths through the place

## Look

- **Art style:** when the concept has a style guide, its written rules for environments, quoted; otherwise the style of the art
- **Materials:** walls, floor, ceiling, and the main furniture, with what each is made of
- **Palette:** five to eight colours, named, in order of area
- **Base light:** the plain, even light its sheet, plan, and views are drawn in

## Props

Props that belong to the place, recur in its shots, and must match. A prop a character handles, or one that matters to the story on its own, is a subject with its own reference, not listed here.

- **{{prop}}:** what it is, its colour and material, and where it sits

- **{{n}}:** where the camera stands, which way it faces, and the shots that use it
