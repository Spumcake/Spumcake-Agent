## Prompt

Voice only, no sound effects and no music. {{voice}} {{non_verbal_before}}{{delivery}}: "{{line}}"{{non_verbal_after}}

## Filling it

From the voice brief: `{{voice}}` is its "For a voice model" description, such as "A tired diner cook in his late fifties at closing time: a low, rough baritone with air in it, slow and plain, unplaced American speech."; `{{delivery}}` the line's delivery, such as "He lets out a short, tired breath through his nose, then says, low, flat, and unhurried, at conversational volume, polite on the surface and weary underneath, with a fraction too much care on the last word"; `{{non_verbal_before}}` and `{{non_verbal_after}}` the voice sounds around the line, or nothing. The example is Last order's approved take (2026-11-13).
