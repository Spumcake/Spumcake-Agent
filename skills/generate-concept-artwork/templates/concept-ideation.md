## Prompt

A bold, ultra-minimalist painting in a loose gouache technique, a concept painting for an animated feature, shows {{premise}} through stylized shapes, dry-brush strokes, big simple shapes of light and shadow, and layered muted complementary colors. It is fully painterly, every area built up from loose, visible, overlapping strokes with varied colour and value within each shape. The variation stays within each layer's own value range: the strokes of the shadowed middle ground stay dark, those of the background stay bright, and the foreground keeps the deepest darks of the picture, so the contrast between the layers stays strong, and where a light shape meets a dark one, the edge stays clear. The strokes are all one uniform size, made with a single broad brush throughout, and long wherever a shape allows, each a long, continuous sweep along the form rather than short dabs, loose and gently curving, following the curves of each form with a relaxed, hand-drawn movement, never ruler-straight or stiff. The staging is planimetric: the camera faces the scene square-on through a long lens, and the scene is built in three flat layers parallel to the picture, like stage flats. The foreground is {{foreground}}, a dark framing shape. The middle ground is {{middle_ground}}. The background is {{background}}, flattened by {{atmosphere}}. Every object faces the camera with its most recognisable side, as a clear, simple silhouette; nothing recedes on a diagonal. {{action}}, each character a clear silhouette against the plainest area behind them, never looking at the camera. {{scale}} The characters are painted with the same brush size as everything else, so they have no more detail than the place. One light, {{light}}, shapes the picture. The light falls on the background, not on the middle ground: the background, {{lit_area}}, is the brightest layer of the picture. The whole middle ground, everything at the characters' distance from the camera, {{middle_objects}} alike, stands in shadow as one dark, low-value layer, almost a silhouette against the bright background, with clear rims of light along the edges that face the light, each a single brushed stroke, bright where it faces the light and fading away from it. The foreground is the darkest layer of all. A soft, {{ambient_colour}} ambient light keeps the shadowed layers readable without lifting them out of shadow. Every object casts a clear shadow away from the light, and the characters are edged with a clear rim light from it, a single brushed stroke along each edge that faces the light, bright where it faces the light and fading away from it, never a continuous outline, their insides in shadow, painted, not cel-shaded. {{second_light}}The colours are {{world_palette}}, while the light and shadow are painted in {{light_palette}}. No text, no lettering, no logos, and no borders.

## Filling it

- `{{premise}}`: the moment in a few words: "an old lighthouse keeper carrying a lantern up the rocks to a lighthouse at dusk".
- `{{foreground}}`, `{{middle_ground}}`, `{{background}}`: the three layers by their objects, each object front-on: "dark, sea-worn rocks along the bottom of the frame"; "a flat shelf of rock with the white lighthouse standing front-on and a small keeper's hut beside it"; "the grey-green sea and a low sky with distant headlands". `{{atmosphere}}`: what flattens the background: "sea haze".
- `{{action}}`: each character, what they wear, where they are in the middle ground, which way they face, and where their eyes go, in profile where it reads: "The keeper, a stooped old man in a yellow oilskin coat, walks in profile across the middle ground to the right, towards the lighthouse door, a lantern held out in front of him, his eyes on the lighthouse door".
- `{{scale}}`: each character's size against the objects around them: "He is small against the lighthouse, no taller than its door."
- `{{light}}`: the one light and where it is, behind or beyond the characters: "the setting sun, low behind the lighthouse on the left". `{{lit_area}}`: the background, where the light falls: "the sunlit sea and sky". `{{middle_objects}}`: everything at the characters' distance, characters and buildings together: "the keeper, the lighthouse, and the hut". `{{ambient_colour}}`: "cool blue-violet".
- `{{second_light}}`: a faint second light, ending with a space, or nothing: "The lighthouse's lamp, just lit, is a small, faint second light, much weaker than the sun. "
- `{{world_palette}}`: the things' own colours: "white-washed stone, slate-grey rock, a faded red door, a yellow oilskin, and a grey-green sea". `{{light_palette}}`: the light's and the shadow's colours: "low golden sunlight and cool blue-violet shadow".
