## Prompt

A bold, ultra-minimalist painting in a loose gouache technique, a concept painting for an animated feature, shows {{premise}} through stylized shapes, dry-brush strokes, big simple shapes of light and shadow, and layered muted complementary colors. The staging is planimetric: the camera faces the scene square-on through a long lens, and the scene is built in three flat layers parallel to the picture, like stage flats. The foreground is {{foreground}}, a dark framing shape. The middle ground is {{middle_ground}}. The background is {{background}}, flattened by {{atmosphere}}. Every object faces the camera with its most recognisable side, as a clear, simple silhouette; nothing recedes on a diagonal. {{action}}, each character a clear silhouette against the plainest area behind them, never looking at the camera. {{scale}} The characters are painted with the same brush size as everything else, so they have no more detail than the place. One light, {{light}}, shapes the picture. The light falls on the background, not on the middle ground: the background, {{lit_area}}, is the brightest layer of the picture. The whole middle ground, everything at the characters' distance from the camera, {{middle_objects}} alike, stands in shadow as one dark, low-value layer, almost a silhouette against the bright background, with only thin rims of light along its edges. The foreground is the darkest layer of all. A soft, {{ambient_colour}} ambient light keeps the shadowed layers readable without lifting them out of shadow. Every object casts a clear shadow away from the light, and the characters are edged with thin rim light from it, their insides in shadow, painted, not cel-shaded. {{second_light}}The colours are {{world_palette}}, while the light and shadow are painted in {{light_palette}}. No text, no lettering, no logos, and no borders.

## Filling it

- `{{premise}}`: the moment in a few words: "an old lighthouse keeper carrying a lantern up the rocks to a lighthouse at dusk".
- `{{foreground}}`, `{{middle_ground}}`, `{{background}}`: the three layers by their objects, each object front-on: "dark, sea-worn rocks along the bottom of the frame"; "a flat shelf of rock with the white lighthouse standing front-on and a small keeper's hut beside it"; "the grey-green sea and a low sky with distant headlands". `{{atmosphere}}`: what flattens the background: "sea haze".
- `{{action}}`: each character, what they wear, where they are in the middle ground, which way they face, and where their eyes go, in profile where it reads: "The keeper, a stooped old man in a yellow oilskin coat, walks in profile across the middle ground to the right, towards the lighthouse door, a lantern held out in his right hand, his eyes on the door".
- `{{scale}}`: each character's size against the objects around them: "He is small against the lighthouse, no taller than its door."
- `{{light}}`: the one light and where it is, behind or beyond the characters: "the setting sun, low behind the lighthouse on the left". `{{lit_area}}`: the background, where the light falls: "the sunlit sea and sky". `{{middle_objects}}`: everything at the characters' distance, characters and buildings together: "the keeper, the lighthouse, and the hut". `{{ambient_colour}}`: "cool blue-violet".
- `{{second_light}}`: a faint second light, ending with a space, or nothing: "The lighthouse's lamp, just lit, is a small, faint second light, much weaker than the sun. "
- `{{world_palette}}`: the things' own colours: "white-washed stone, slate-grey rock, a faded red door, a yellow oilskin, and a grey-green sea". `{{light_palette}}`: the light's and the shadow's colours: "low golden sunlight and cool blue-violet shadow".
