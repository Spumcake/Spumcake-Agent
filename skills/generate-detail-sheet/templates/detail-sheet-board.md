## Prompt

The first attached image is the finished concept painting of {{subject}}. The second attached image is {{its}} animatic expression sheet. Draw a level-of-detail sheet of {{it}} in the second image's partially completed digital line art: the main forms drawn with clean, confident, single lines in dark grey-black, the line's weight varying a little, and only here and there a light construction stroke still showing, as if the inking is half done, with no fills, no shading, and no grey tones, on a plain white page, with no colour. The same {{subject_short}}, full body, {{pose}}, four times side by side from left to right, standing on one shared ground line, as {{it}} would look at four distances from the camera. The first, on the left, is close: {{its}} full height fills almost the whole height of the sheet. The second is at a middle distance, half that height. The third is far, a quarter of that height. The fourth is very far, an eighth of that height. Draw the whole sheet with one and the same pen width, so the further {{it}} is, the fewer lines {{it}} is made of: close, {{close_marks}}; at the middle distance, {{middle_marks}}; far, {{far_marks}}; very far, {{very_far_marks}}. No labels, no text, no background, and nothing else on the sheet.

## Filling it

As [the painted detail sheet](detail-sheet.md), with `{{pose}}` a relaxed pose that shows the character, such as "standing in a relaxed, heavy stance", and the marks written as lines, not strokes of paint.
